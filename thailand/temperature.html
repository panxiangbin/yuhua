หลักการทำงานและโครงสร้าง

  • Circulation pump drives heat-transfer fluid through a sealed loop between the unit and external equipment such as a jacketed reactor.
  • Heating and refrigeration act on the same circulation medium; the controller continuously adjusts output to track process temperature.
  • The separate expansion vessel is thermally isolated so its medium remains closer to ambient, reducing high-temperature oil fumes and low-temperature moisture ingress.
  • Manual states the system is designed for reactor temperature control, rotary-evaporator temperature control and process simulation in chemical, pharmaceutical and materials applications.
Operating note: correct heat-transfer fluid must remain liquid across the selected temperature range. Extreme temperature changes should be gradual; the manual recommends avoiding sudden large steps and frequent refrigeration/heating switching.

Product principle / use

  • Mechanical refrigeration cools the reservoir through an internal coil; the circulation pump sends the cold medium to an external reactor jacket or condenser.
  • Can support double-layer glass reactors, rotary evaporators, condenser coils and immersed low-temperature experiments.
  • Manual lists common media such as high-purity ethanol, glycol/water mixture or dedicated coolant; actual medium must suit the required temperature and process safety.
