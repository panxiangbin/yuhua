Microwave heating / product features from manual

  • Microwave heating converts electromagnetic energy into heat in microwave-absorbing materials, enabling rapid internal/external heating without relying solely on surface heat transfer.
  • Manual describes fast response, rapid start/stop and output adjustment, volumetric heating and material-selective heating based on dielectric loss.
  • Adaptive PID control is described as automatically adjusting control parameters as environment, reaction volume and material properties change.
  • PTFE-coated temperature sensor is designed to reduce electromagnetic interference and improve resistance to acid, alkali and organic solutions.
  • Five-stage mode lets each stage have independent maximum power, temperature and time settings.
Power-version difference: the detailed manual lists ≤900W microwave output, while the current 2026 commercial/spec table lists ≤750W with a 0–800W adjustment field. For a customer order, the confirmed current production configuration / quotation is the final reference.
