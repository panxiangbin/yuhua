คุณสมบัติระบบตามคู่มือ

  • PTFE + rubber composite sealing system and vacuum gauge for vacuum monitoring.
  • Vertical multi-pass condenser improves condensation and solvent recovery.
  • PID digital display for bath temperature and digital speed display.
  • Electric bath lift provides smooth immersion / removal of the rotating flask.
  • Collection bottle has an independent discharge structure for solvent recovery.
  • Vacuum switching / vent arrangement supports multi-step collection and handling.
Important distinction: the source manual lists glass temperature resistance -80…+250°C, while the actual water-bath control range is ambient…99°C. The website now keeps these two fields separate so customers do not confuse them.
