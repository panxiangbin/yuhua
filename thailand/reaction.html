โครงสร้างและฟังก์ชันตามคู่มือ

  • Material-contact components use high-borosilicate glass and PTFE to improve chemical compatibility.
  • System supports stirring, condensation, controlled addition, reflux and collection; jacket accepts hot/cold circulating media.
  • Can operate at atmospheric or negative pressure when connected to a suitable vacuum source.
  • Glass + PTFE side/bottom discharge structure is designed to reduce retained liquid.
  • Can be paired with circulating-water / diaphragm vacuum pumps and high-low-temperature circulators.
Operating limit: glass-reactor pressure, temperature and filling limits are safety parameters. Do not infer pressure capability from another reactor series. Customized ports or explosion-proof versions require their own datasheet.
